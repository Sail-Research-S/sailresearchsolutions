Why SAIL

Research support with clear boundaries.

Every service begins with the research objective, current document status and expected output rather than treating the task as generic document editing.

Client research materials are handled for the agreed service purpose and should not be publicly disclosed without authorization.

SAIL does not support fabricated data, false authorship, falsified results or guaranteed publication claims.

Scope, timeline and payment should be confirmed before work begins. The payment page is intended for approved quotations or communicated amounts.
