SAIL Research Solutions — Categorised Research Tools Layout

Research Tools are now organised into categories with icons:
- Sample Size & Study Planning
- Epidemiology & Risk Measures
- Diagnostic Research
- Statistics & Effect Size
- Clinical & Pharmacy Calculators
- Research Utilities
- Manuscript & Writing
- References & Literature
- Reporting Checklists

Categories remain collapsed by default, so all calculators are not visible at once.

When a tool opens:
- only that tool expands
- it becomes full-width inside its category
- neighbouring tools retain their natural compact size
- another open tool in the same category closes automatically

Search automatically opens only categories containing matching tools.
All existing calculator and checklist logic is retained.
