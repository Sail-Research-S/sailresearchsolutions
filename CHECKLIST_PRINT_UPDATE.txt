SAIL Research Solutions — Checklist Print Update

Added Print Checklist to every interactive reporting checklist.

The print output includes:
- checklist title
- current checked/unchecked items
- completed item count
- completion percentage
- print date/time
- SAIL Research Solutions heading

Only the selected checklist is printed. Users can also choose Save as PDF
from the browser print dialog.

Existing categorized Research Tools layout and calculator behavior are unchanged.
